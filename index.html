Printer fit

Your model on each build plate, to scale. The wireframe shows the printer’s height limit.

Purple branches are a coarse estimate: 45° overhang threshold, 40° branch limit, build-plate only. This is not PrusaSlicer output. Use your printer’s slicer profile for actual supports; support clearance is not included in the fit result.

STL units are assumed to be millimeters. Fit checks use the model’s bounds in its original or 90° orientations, independent of the camera view. Supports, bed adhesion, and printer settings may need extra room.
